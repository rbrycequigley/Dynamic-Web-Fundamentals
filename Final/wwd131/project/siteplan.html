Bryce Quigley

WDD 131

Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to act as a visual database for my decks in the Magic the Gathering card game. I will do this by obtaining card information from a MTG API. I then will use arrays to store and load data about each deck as needed.

Audience

The audience is me, whatever employeer may be looking at my GitHub, and perhaps whatever MTG nerd wants to take a look at the decks I have made.

Dynamic elements

I will prioritize 'phone first' display and dynamically change between it and a desktop mode. Handheld mode will allow users to view one card per row."

Desktop mode will allow users to view up to 5 cards per row.

Wireframe

https://sites.google.com/byui.edu/dynamicwebfun-siteplan/home
